# Emberwake — Story checkpoints

Where the game is up to, one screenshot per story checkpoint. Generated by
`tools/checkpoints.sh` from `data/scenarios.json` (at commit `78132be`, 2026-11-13 09:21 UTC).

Play from any of them: `godot --path . -- --scenario=<id>`, or in a debug build open the
pause menu (Esc) → **Chapter select (dev)**. `--scenario=list` prints them all.
Screenshots come from the sandbox's Compatibility renderer and look paler than the game.

## Act II — The Drift

### Thornwold Landing  (`--scenario=thornwold`)

Just ashore after the crossing, with Pell along. Meet Bram Kettle at the lumber camp and look at the bramble wall across the cart road.

![Thornwold Landing](checkpoints/thornwold.png)

### Past the bramble wall  (`--scenario=thornwold_woods`)

Bram met; the ember parted the bramble wall. The straight road into the Greying, the waymark lanterns west to the colliers' clearing, Hob Marl and his clamp. Bram's salt not yet carried.

![Past the bramble wall](checkpoints/thornwold_woods.png)

### The ridge and the Lamp  (`--scenario=thornwold_ridge`)

Hob met and paid; the Keepers' ladder stood and climbed. The ridge top in the Greying: the cold Ridge Light, the keeper's lodge, and the Lamp at the lantern bench. Ask about the iron box, the ladder, Aldous, and a lantern for the stair.

![The ridge and the Lamp](checkpoints/thornwold_ridge.png)

### The Ridge Light lit  (`--scenario=thornwold_lit`)

Thornwold's burning, the boots path: the Lamp met and asked; Hob gave Ottie's boots (his memory of the four colliers) and they burned in the Ridge Light. The tower gold, the fog leaning back. Go down to the woods and the camp: Hob comes in by daylight, Bram, Pell and Oda after the burn.

![The Ridge Light lit](checkpoints/thornwold_lit.png)

### Glasswater Fen  (`--scenario=glasswater_fen`)

Oda has sailed north-about from the lit Ridge Light to Glasswater Fen (boots path; Hob came in). Come ashore at the staithe: Hesper Vail of the Unmoored, Dunstan's rowboat, the letting post past the west walk, Corran Teal the eel-man, and the Heron Light dark out on the mere.

![Glasswater Fen](checkpoints/glasswater_fen.png)

### Out to the Heron's legs  (`--scenario=heron_mere`)

Hesper met (the ember kept bare); Corran asked for his far trap, it lifted from the end of the long walk and brought back (in it, the keeper's skiff bell), and he has poled you out to the Heron Light. The Foot, the keeper's skiff (hang the bell or keep it), the scraped ladder, a big man at the fog's edge, Corran waiting by the punt.

![Out to the Heron's legs](checkpoints/heron_mere.png)

### The Heron Light, lit  (`--scenario=heron_lit`)

Back from the legs, Hesper told of a small light that goes up the Heron's ladder some nights; Corran held the foot on a dry day and you climbed to the cradle (cold put-down things, a whittled gull, the keeper's coat folded). Hesper gave her name; it fed the Heron. Lit, the fog leaning back off the mere; Corran waits to pole you back by its light; the bell hung back on her bow.

![The Heron Light, lit](checkpoints/heron_lit.png)

### For after: Dunstan found  (`--scenario=dunstan_found`)

Poled back by the Heron's light; the lantern lit at the fog's foot sits at the end of the long walk. Walk out along the boards: Dunstan Tollen on a tussock with his boots in the mere, whittling, the lantern lit beside him (Mara's word was left on the post, so a bit of red cord at his belt).

![For after: Dunstan found](checkpoints/dunstan_found.png)

### East-about to Wrackholm  (`--scenario=wrackholm`)

Dunstan found (Mara's word carried: a gull for the stool; the rule kept). Oda sailed east-about from the lit fen and nobody answered her horn; she came in by soundings past the Teeth. The Tidewrights' yard is dark, the Gannet hauled up on the hard, the answering horn lashed shut by the lane-house door, Wenna Garrow sitting by it, Jory Pask stripping the Gannet's copper.

![East-about to Wrackholm](checkpoints/wrackholm.png)

### The Teeth at the bottom of the springs  (`--scenario=the_teeth`)

The Gannet's crew named into the lashed horn (Jory told: it was Wenna's answer). Wenna remembered the white: nobody knew the Teeth by heart, you put your feet on the lampwardens' white-lead marks. Waited the spring tide down at the spit's end; the ember brought the marks back rock to rock; crossed on the white. Walk the reef road west to the Wrack's stack and climb to its lantern room.

![The Teeth at the bottom of the springs](checkpoints/the_teeth.png)

### The Wrack lit, the horn answered  (`--scenario=wrack_lit`)

Wenna and Jory gave together (her answer, his pot); the Gannet's board stayed on her transom. Hale's white-lead pot burned in the Wrack's cradle: the Wrack Light burns gold over the Teeth, the fog leaning back; Jory no longer remembers going out on the white. Talk to Wenna to unlash and sound the answering horn, open the lane-house Book, and be sworn a friend of the lanes.

![The Wrack lit, the horn answered](checkpoints/wrack_lit.png)

### The Lamp's name carried home  (`--scenario=lamp_named`)

The horn answered, the Book opened (the Lamp's page read: WYNN SALLOW), sworn a friend of the lanes; Oda sailed back west by the fen to Thornwold. On the ridge the player said the name outright: the Lamp has put its hood back and remembers the novice on the east stair who told it to keep a coal back. Oda, at the landing, has a word for a sworn friend carrying fares home.

![The Lamp's name carried home](checkpoints/lamp_named.png)

### The East Stair  (`--scenario=east_stair`)

Oda sailed the player back from Thornwold to Saltmarrow. On his bench by the Wrens' steps Aldous heard that Wynn Sallow is alive, counted six novices on the east stair by name and a seventh light with no face, and was asked 'could it have been me?' He couldn't find the player's face anywhere. He told of the novices' roll in the warden's niche on Cindermoor and laid the Wrens' brazier for whoever comes down the stair.

![The East Stair](checkpoints/east_stair.png)

### The Fifth  (`--scenario=the_fifth`)

The salt-row path instead of the boots (Thornwold's burning re-chosen for this checkpoint): Bram's camp no longer knows the colliers, Hob still keeps the four. Back on Thornwold after the east stair, Hob was asked up the ladder from the tally-house step (Pell gave his count-stick back); on the ridge the Lamp told him he is the fifth and gave him a lantern of his own lit off the Ridge Light. He stands at the ladder's head with it, waiting on the player's word: go after the four, or stay at the bottom of the road.

![The Fifth](checkpoints/the_fifth.png)

### The Walk Back  (`--scenario=the_walk_back`)

After the east stair Oda sailed the player back to the fen. Dunstan's lantern had come halfway in along the long walk and stopped (the boards are laid for going in); the player walked ahead of him with the ember, and he stands at the staithe's foot in front of Hesper to ask for his name himself. Talk to him: Hesper gives it back, and the player's word sends him home to Saltmarrow or keeps him turning the boards.

### Word on the Water  (`--scenario=word_on_the_water`)

After The Walk Back, Dunstan rowed home to Saltmarrow facing backward and sits on his stool by Mara's door; Tam rang the gate bell for him, Hesk saw the Tollen shoulders come home, Aldous watched from his bench. The player carried Dunstan's word to Oda ("not laughing at the weather") and the Slow Mercy lies at the dock. Oda has sent word back: his berth's still swung. ### The Northing  (`--scenario=the_northing`)

After The Fifth (Hob gone up the old road with his lantern), the player walked the old Keepers' road north along Thornwold's spine to the Northing, the Keepers' fire-cairn on the north cliff, and found Ottie Swale keeping a charcoal fire in its basket for the three who went down to the far shore. The Lamp lit a lantern off the Ridge Light for him. Carry it up the spine and give it to Ottie: hang it on the Northing (he goes home to his boots) or put it in his hand (he takes it down the steps).

![The Northing](checkpoints/the_northing.png)

### The Far Shore  (`--scenario=the_far_shore`)

After The Northing (Ottie took the Ridge Light's lantern down the Keepers' steps; Hob went down before him), the player went down the steps to the far shore under the Northing's cliff and found Wenna Coll on the end of the Keepers' boat-stage waiting for the Spire's boat, the Drays arguing by a driftwood fire, Ottie under the lantern he hung on the stage's bracket and Hob counting heads. Tell Wenna what you think: come up (the Lamp breaks the warm side of its kept coal for their hearths) or wait (and tell the Spire they're there).

![The Far Shore](checkpoints/the_far_shore.png)

### The Bend in the Road  (`--scenario=the_bend_in_the_road`)

After The Far Shore (the three came up; the Lamp broke the warm side of its coal for their hearths), Wenna Coll walked out at first light with her lantern and found the old bend in the cart road the Snuffing straightened: her hearth, a cold hut, a clamp burned clean through while she walked round it. Help her decide where her one light goes: on the bend's waymark (the road a road again) or by her own door (a light of her own).

![The Bend in the Road](checkpoints/the_bend_in_the_road.png)

### Kindleby  (`--scenario=kindleby`)

After the Wrack was lit, Oda told of the lane on: south-about down the Taper to Kindleby, the Keepers' mother-house. The quay bell answered before she blew; the island is ringed with candles and the fog stands a cable's width off. Brother Tobin at the bell; Provost Agathe Lorn at the chapter-house, who will ask the Wakebearer to bow to the rite.

![Kindleby](checkpoints/kindleby.png)

### Kindleby's burning  (`--scenario=kindleby_lit`)

The Wakebearer held the ring from the crown while the chapter sat; the chapter chose Kindleby's night of the boats, old Brother Anselm carried the lantern into the fog, and the Wakebearer (having bowed to the rite) burned it in the Vigil Light. The fog leans back off the island; nobody on Kindleby remembers how the old ones came over.

![Kindleby's burning](checkpoints/kindleby_lit.png)

### The skiff at the stage  (`--scenario=skiff_in`)

The colliers were told to wait on the Northing's stage, and the Provost of Kindleby was told of them; while the chapter sat, the chapter's skiff rowed north. It's tied up at the stage's ring now, Sister Hild and Sister Pim with oil, meal and a lantern, and Wenna Coll wants telling: home, or Kindleby.

![The skiff at the stage](checkpoints/skiff_in.png)

### Colliers on the candles  (`--scenario=colliers_on_kindleby`)

Wenna told to go with the sisters: everyone on the far shore got into Kindleby's skiff. Wenna and Abe keep candles on the west wall, Tolly on the east by the Quiet House, Hob counts them all, and Ottie looks north from the quay.

![Colliers on the candles](checkpoints/colliers_on_kindleby.png)

### What the ember keeps  (`--scenario=the_stair_taper`)

Kindleby lit: as the Vigil Light caught, its smoke closed round the Wakebearer's hand and left a notched novice's stair-taper in it, humming with a memory of the Wakebearer's own. The Provost, Brother Tobin and (on Saltmarrow) Brother Aldous know what it is.

![What the ember keeps](checkpoints/the_stair_taper.png)

### The Low Water  (`--scenario=lowsong`)

Oda told of the ring's southern gap; the Provost said nobody goes out in the wet, and then yes; Hild rowed the Wakebearer out from Kindleby into the singing fog of the Low Water to Lowsong's landing. The light on the rock is cold, and its Keeper is standing under it with a small lamp.

![The Low Water](checkpoints/lowsong.png)

### A light for Lowsong  (`--scenario=lowsong_lit`)

Brother Iver put his Kindleby down into his cell key with the ember beside him, and it fed Lowsong's light: he keeps the rock now, listening, and doesn't know there was anywhere else. The singing went off past the skerries, and the ember gave back a second memory of the east stair: a novice's bone hood-pin.

![A light for Lowsong](checkpoints/lowsong_lit.png)

### The lights between  (`--scenario=the_lane_in`)

Hild rowed the Wakebearer back from Lowsong's lit rock. From the Slow Mercy's rail Oda counted six lights, the lights between, and inked one line on her chart: the lane in, from Kindleby's quay to Cindermoor. Ask her to take you in, and Act II ends.

![The lights between](checkpoints/the_lane_in.png)

## Act III — The Spire

### The Boat Stair  (`--scenario=the_boat_stair`)

Oda took the Wakebearer in on the morning ebb, down the lane in, steering by the six lights astern. Cindermoor came up out of the fog: black cinder, brown heather, and the Hearthspire dark on the crown. At the Boat Stair a Hushed boat-warden counts the slips: seven out, one in.

![The Boat Stair](checkpoints/the_boat_stair.png)

### The Long Steps  (`--scenario=the_long_steps`)

The cinder road forgot where it went a few yards up from the quay. Two cressets lit from the ember made it remember up the notch and over the lip, and Cindermoor goes on up in long steps into the thickest fog on the ring, a cold cresset at every turn.

![The Long Steps](checkpoints/the_long_steps.png)

### The Unmoored on the Shoulder  (`--scenario=the_unmoored`)

Four cressets up the long steps, lit one by one; the last one's light runs along the shoulder under the crown and stops short at the Unmoored, who came to Cindermoor on purpose because the fog is thickest here. The next cresset up the road has been put out on purpose.

![The Unmoored on the Shoulder](checkpoints/the_unmoored.png)

### The Spire's Foot  (`--scenario=the_spire_foot`)

The doused cresset dug out and relit over the Unmoored's wish; they went off along the shoulder into the fog, and the road remembered itself up to the crown, where the fog stops round the Hearthspire's foot. The east stair door stands ajar beside the warden's niche.

![The Spire's Foot](checkpoints/the_spire_foot.png)

### The Chapter Comes In  (`--scenario=the_chapter`)

Back down off the moor from the Hearthspire's foot, the roll read: the first of the seven Spire boats has come in to the Boat Stair under oars, with the Provost of Kindleby and what of the chapter she could put in a boat, to do it properly. Merrit held the bow.

![The Chapter Comes In](checkpoints/the_chapter.png)

### The Chapter Sits  (`--scenario=the_sitting`)

The Provost said when, and the chapter went up the lit road at dusk to sit at the Spire's foot in a ring of candles. The Long Book has every island's burning for nine hundred years and not one line for the Spire; under Aldous's seat in the niche a stair goes down to the lower rooms. Tomorrow, the Provost says. Nobody goes down before.

![The Chapter Sits](checkpoints/the_sitting.png)

### The Spire's Own Book  (`--scenario=the_lower_rooms`)

Morning after the sitting: the chapter went down twelve steps and a turn under the warden's seat to the Spire's lower rooms and read its own book: nine hundred midwinters of the Hushed, given to the flame with all that was in them. The last page is the High Keeper's, Oriel Sayre's: the flame was starved on purpose. The Provost wrote the Spire's line into the Long Book.

![The Spire's Own Book](checkpoints/the_lower_rooms.png)

### The Woman on the Ring Path  (`--scenario=the_eighth_boat`)

The Provost reckoned the High Keeper never left Cindermoor, and the chapter came back up from the lower rooms with the Wakebearer to look. A woman walks the worn path round the Spire's foot by daylight, her robe gone grey to the shoulders, and doesn't look at the east stair door. The Provost said 'Oriel', and she didn't turn.

![The Woman on the Ring Path](checkpoints/the_eighth_boat.png)

### The Nearest Voice  (`--scenario=the_nearest_voice`)

The Wakebearer told Oriel Sayre who she was, and she sat down. In the quiet, singing came down the east stair, and the nearest voice got two words out: 'Not Hushed.' Down in the lower rooms the Provost found the one line in the Spire's book that says so, and the name scraped off it and kept on the story: Isolde Vane, the last Wakebearer. She's still singing at the door.

![The Nearest Voice](checkpoints/the_nearest_voice.png)

### The Last Landing  (`--scenario=the_last_landing`)

The Wakebearer said Isolde Vane's name up the east stair, and she said yes. Then they lit the Stair-Taper from the ember and went up, counting, seven landings to the last one under the lantern room, and the count finished, and the rest came back: the High Keeper with the last coal, a novice who asked to carry it, a hood pinned so nobody would see whose face went down the stair.

![The Last Landing](checkpoints/the_last_landing.png)

### What She Was to You  (`--scenario=what_she_was`)

Down from the last landing with the seventh name written back on the roll, the Wakebearer told Oriel Sayre it was them she gave the last coal to, and asked her up the stair. She waits by the east stair door with her hand not quite on the frame.

![What She Was to You](checkpoints/what_she_was.png)

### The Lantern Room  (`--scenario=the_lantern_room`)

The chapter sat once more at the Spire's foot by daylight and wrote that it does not choose. Up past seven, with Oriel Sayre a step behind, the Wakebearer stands in the lantern room: the cradle cold, the panes black, and the Hollow Choir round it, listening.

![The Lantern Room](checkpoints/the_lantern_room.png)

### The Spire Lit  (`--scenario=the_spire_lit`)

At the cradle the Wakebearer put the ember in the ash. The Firstflame burns in the lantern room again, the panes gold through the soot, and the Hollow Choir stands in close round it, warm. It took the hand that lit it first.

![The Spire Lit](checkpoints/the_spire_lit.png)

### The Homecoming  (`--scenario=the_homecoming`)

After the lit ending Oda sailed the lane out by the Spire's light astern. On Kindleby's quay Tobin rang the bell's second stroke, the one for coming back, and low in the west the Hearthspire burns gold over the water.

![The Homecoming](checkpoints/the_homecoming.png)

### The Wrens' Fire  (`--scenario=the_wrens_fire`)

In the dark ending the Mercy put in at Saltmarrow with the ember still burning in the Wakebearer's hand. Mara called the village to write its own midwinter list, and by the Wrens' steps the brazier Aldous kept laid for whoever came down the stair burns at last.

![The Wrens' Fire](checkpoints/the_wrens_fire.png)

### The Two Keepers  (`--scenario=the_two_keepers`)

After the lit ending Aldous kept the door-warden's answer and crossed, Thornwold first. He climbed the Keepers' ladder ahead of the Wakebearer, and on the lantern bench under the Ridge Light he said his sorry to Wynn Sallow in person, the day the lanes run with nobody counting. The Hearthspire burns gold in the east.

![The Two Keepers](checkpoints/the_two_keepers.png)

### The Staithe Lantern  (`--scenario=the_staithe_lantern`)

After the lit ending the Mercy sailed round to Glasswater Fen. Hesper met it with a lantern on a pole, the way the fen folk used to meet every boat; the fog has gone thin as breath on the pools, and the Hearthspire burns gold in the south past the reeds. Dunstan stayed, and is turning the long walk's boards.

![The Staithe Lantern](checkpoints/the_staithe_lantern.png)
